Biggest takeaways from the data

  • Rebills fail 2 in 3 times. 31.6% approval, against 83.4% when the fan is present. They are 27% of payments but 38% of declined value. Creators lost $25.6k on rebills, more than they earned from them.
  • One provider is 11 points better. On identical traffic (US card, fan‑initiated, mainstream bank, verified address) Gateway 1 approves 93.5% against 82.8%. Inside Gateway 2, merchant_E beats merchant_C on the same traffic.
  • Weak card and address data costs approvals. Unverified addresses approve 57% against 88%. Fintech and neobank cards approve 16% against 49% on merchant‑initiated charges.

Quick win: re‑route similar traffic to channels with higher approval rates

The providers serve different fans: only Gateway 2 takes Latin America and most of the rest of the world. So I only compare where they overlap, holding everything else equal: US card · fan‑initiated · mainstream bank · full address match.

Gateway 1 vs Gateway 2 Quick win 1

93.5%Gateway 1 · 1,804 payments
82.8%Gateway 2 · 907 payments

A 10.7‑point gap on identical traffic, statistically solid. The same banks approve more on Gateway 1 (e.g. Bank of America, US fan‑initiated: 96% vs 77%).

Action: route this segment to Gateway 1 as a test. Up to ~$6.2k / month more approved value.

merchant_E vs merchant_C (both Gateway 2) Quick win 2

84.6%merchant_E · 611 payments
79.1%merchant_C · 296 payments

Same provider, same countries, same $40 average, same card and address mix. Across all fan‑initiated payments the gap is 80.1% vs 71.4%.

Action: ask the provider why merchant_C’s setup differs, or move its volume to merchant_E. Up to ~$2.2k / month.

Check before moving volume: provider fees, contract volume commitments and each provider’s appetite for adult merchants aren’t in the data. Gateway 2 may also receive Gateway 1’s retries, which would make its traffic harder, and we can’t check without a fan ID.

Recommendations

1 · Smarter retries

Retry insufficient‑funds declines around payday, with a cap. Stop retrying hard stops and cancellations: it costs fees and breaks card‑scheme retry rules.

$38.1k retry‑later · $13.6k never retry

2 · Fix card details before we charge

Capture the full billing address and use the $0 card check to catch bad details. For fintech cards, use account updater and remind fans before renewal.

$11.3k weak address · $12.0k fintech

3 · Use both providers

Run the two quick wins on page 4. Retry “do not honour” once on the other provider. Review setup errors with each provider.

$15.3k reroute · $4.9k setup
Measure each one against a holdout: recovered value within 7 days, plus chargebacks, fraud, retries per payment and cancellations.

Definitions

Fan‑initiatedThe fan is present and starts the payment: tips, pay‑per‑view, new subscriptions.
First subscription chargeFan‑present, but it also sets up future merchant charges. Gateway 1 labels it merchant‑initiated (“Initial MIT”); Gateway 2 labels it C. Grouped as fan‑initiated: 3‑D Secure present, 81% approval.
Subscription rebillA monthly charge to a saved card without the fan present.
Fintech / neobankCards from banks that issue for fintech, neobank and prepaid programmes: Sutton, Bancorp, Stride, Green Dot, Pathward (MetaBank). Based on industry knowledge.
Mainstream bankAny other named bank. “Unknown” = no bank name (28% of Gateway 2), not shown.
Address checkFull or ZIP match; partial or no match; not verified or none returned.
Merchant accountOnlyFans’ account at each provider, chosen by the card’s billing country. Key = provider + account.
